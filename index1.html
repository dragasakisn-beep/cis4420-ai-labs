Responsible testing · Rules of engagement

Use only designated challenge environments, follow each platform's rules, and never target third-party services or real data. Huntr's challenge rules prohibit automation; attacks must be entered manually. Capture your observations and recommended safeguards for class discussion. Your learning pathway

From concept to secure design

Use this simple workflow to turn hands-on exploration into a security engineering outcome.

01 / EXPLORE

Understand the system

Identify the AI assistant's purpose, instructions, data sources, and available tools.

02 / TEST

Observe the trust boundary

Perform only permitted exercises. Note where untrusted input influences outputs or actions.

03 / DEFEND

Recommend controls

Identify practical mitigations such as least privilege, input isolation, and validation.
